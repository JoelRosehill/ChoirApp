# Koris — member and director screens

Scope: every server-rendered screen (name entry, event list, event page, director). Mode: Operate. Phone-first; opened from group-chat links and from the home-screen PWA.

Audience and job: singers answer "Būšu / Nebūšu" in one tap; the director creates an event, shares its link, and sees whether it reaches its minimum. States that matter: unanswered, answered yes/no, minimum reached ("Notiks"), short of minimum, past, empty list, notifications off/on/unsupported, iPhone not yet installed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Attendance is woven, not measured. Each event carries a woven band that gains one unit per "Būšu" until it closes at the minimum. Refuses the category default of a grey RSVP card with a percentage bar.

OWN-WORLD: Three values: unbleached linen ground, near-black ink, and madder red, plus a forest green used only for the reached state. A madder header band whose lower edge is a woven strip. One sturdy grotesque at every level. Spacing on a 4px weave-pick grid. Squared corners softened to 3px; hairlines at one device pixel.

STORY: The member sees who they are, what is waiting for their answer, and answers in place. The director sees each event's band filling and knows on sight whether it happens.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Madder header with the choir name and the member's name. "Gaida tavu atbildi" list: date block, title, time and place, the woven band with "6 / 10", and two full-width Būšu / Nebūšu buttons. Primary action at thumb height.

FORM: Woven belt (josta), candidate 4 of 7, seed:5548f013bdad9749. Raises: three-value palette (from centre-rail reference setting); one weave-unit grid (from Crouwel grid specimen); exact count, one unit per singer, never a percentage (from labanotation); answered rows carry the member's mark (from convention catalogue); nothing disappears, it cancels: a deleted event notifies instead of vanishing silently (from ticket wallet).
